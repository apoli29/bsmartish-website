## Design Context

### Project
BSMARTISH — an urban-renovation studio operating in Porto since 2017 (originally Spain, 1997). The site presents finished, curated mid-term rental properties and offers consultancy to owners commissioning their own renovation.

### Users
Three audiences must be served by the same page:
1. **Future residents** — people looking to live in a BSMARTISH property (mid-term rental).
2. **Buyers / investors** — capital looking for finished, high-yield urban properties.
3. **Project owners** — owners of buildings who want BSMARTISH to develop their renovation project.

Visitors arrive curious, comparison-shopping, and somewhat skeptical. They need: trust, evidence of craftsmanship, clarity about *which* of the three relationships applies to them.

### Brand Personality
**Calm, confident, refined, urban.** Not loud. Not luxury-luxury (no gold, no serif flourish). Instead: precise, considered, slightly architectural. Think: a well-edited interiors magazine published by a studio that also does the work.

**Three words:** assured, urbane, considered.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Editorial-architectural:** photography-led, generous whitespace, large headings, restrained motion.
- Cohesion with the existing Home page is non-negotiable. Same fonts, same palette, same section rhythm, same FadeIn cadence, same border colour.
- Modern UI moments — frosted glass cards with 3D tilt + glare — used as a **signature element**, not as wallpaper. Three glass moments per page maximum.
- Anti-reference: generic real-estate listing sites (heavy, gradient-text, identical card grids, "+ Get a quote" CTAs everywhere).

### Theme
**Light by default** (Canvas White surfaces). One **dark Deep Urban moment** per long page is allowed for gravity (e.g. Mission/Vision, hero overlays). Never default to dark mode globally.

### Palette (CSS variables in `globals.css`)
| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--color-canvas-white` | `#f8f8f8` | Default page background |
| `--color-deep-urban` | `#202831` | Body text, dark sections, dark glass tone, primary buttons |
| `--color-slate-blue` | `#6b87a4` | **All section H2 headings**, all eyebrow labels, accent lines, hover/focus accents |
| `--color-slate-gray` | `#75797c` | Secondary/muted text only (e.g. small labels under stats) |

**Hard rule (locked into auto-memory):** every section H2 must be `#6b87a4` in Radnika. Never Deep Urban for H2s.

### Typography (loaded as local fonts in `app/layout.js`)
| Token | Font | Weight | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--font-radnika` | Radnika Medium | 500 | All H1, H2, H3 headlines (always Slate Blue for H2) |
| `--font-garet` | Garet Heavy | 800 | Display numerals only — stat values, big numbered labels (01-04, etc.) |
| `--font-aileron` | Aileron | 400 / 600 / 700 | Body (400), eyebrows + button labels + bold accents (600), occasional emphasis (700) |

**Eyebrow pattern:** Aileron 600, uppercase, `tracking-[0.15em]` to `[0.22em]`, ~`text-[0.7rem]`, Slate Blue — optionally preceded by a `28px × 1px` Slate Blue line.

### Layout & Rhythm
- **Container:** `max-w-screen-xl mx-auto px-8 md:px-14 lg:px-20`
- **Section vertical padding:** `pt-16 md:pt-20 lg:pt-24 pb-20 md:pb-24 lg:pb-28` (vary slightly per section for visual rhythm)
- **Inter-section borders:** `borderTop: '1px solid #e4e4e4'` for adjacent Canvas-White sections
- **Reveal motion:** wrap blocks in `<FadeIn delay={0|150|250|400}>` — 750ms cubic-bezier, translateY 16px → 0
- **Card identity:** `rounded-3xl shadow-2xl` on glass cards; smaller `rounded` on photo tiles

### Design Principles
1. **Restraint over ornament.** White space and confident typography do more than gradients and badges.
2. **Photography is the protagonist.** Glass and typography frame it; never compete.
3. **One signature touch, used sparingly.** The interactive frosted-glass card with mouse-tracked glare is the brand's UI moment — don't dilute it by repeating it everywhere.
4. **Cohesion before novelty.** Every new page should feel like a continuation of Home, not a reinvention.
5. **Three audiences, one voice.** Copy must work for residents, investors, and project owners without splitting tone.

### Anti-references (do not produce)
- Identical card grids (4 icon+heading+text cards, repeated)
- Side-stripe colored borders on cards (`border-left: 4px solid …`)
- Gradient text (`background-clip: text` on a gradient)
- Generic glassmorphism applied to every container
- Inter / Roboto / "AI startup" sans-serif defaults — typography is locked to Radnika + Garet + Aileron
- Bouncy/elastic motion easings
